Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2.75 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1.25 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2.5 sticks unsalted butter, softened
  • 1.25 cups packed dark-brown sugar
  • 0.75 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1.5 cups semisweet chocolate chips

Baking Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Combine or sift flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda in a bowl.
  3. Beat butter and sugars with a mixer on medium-high speed until pale and fluffy (about 4 minutes).
  4. Beat in eggs and vanilla extract, then reduce speed to low.
  5. Add the flour mixture; beat until just combined. Mix in chocolate chips.
  6. Drop about 3 tablespoon dough balls onto parchment-lined baking sheets, about 2 inches apart.
  7. Bake until golden around the edges but soft in the middle (about 15 minutes).
  8. Let cool on the baking sheets for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
